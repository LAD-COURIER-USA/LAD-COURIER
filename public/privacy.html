1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how **LAD DIGITAL SYSTEMS LLC** ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information through the LAD Courier mobile application and its SaaS platform.

2. Data We Collect

  • Precise Location: We collect foreground and background GPS data to enable the "Radar" mission system and provide real-time tracking for security.
  • Camera and Images: We access the camera for identity verification (Liveness Selfie) and for capturing delivery evidence (receipts/package photos).
  • Biometric Information: We utilize local biometric hardware (fingerprint/face ID) for secure login. We DO NOT store or see your raw biometric data; it stays on your device.
  • Contact Data: Names and phone numbers for communication between drivers and clients during missions.

3. Sovereign Data Policy (36-Hour Window)

LAD Courier operates under a privacy-first model:

Operational data (locations, images, and chat details) are stored only for a strict period of 36 hours for audit and dispute resolution. After this window, detailed logs are automatically purged from our active systems.

4. Third-Party Services

We use industry-standard partners to secure your operations:

  • Stripe: For secure, bank-grade payment processing.
  • Google Maps: For routing and geofencing.
  • Firebase: For cloud data hosting and security.
